For some time now, Akagi and Kaga have been acting suspiciosly, but Zuikaku and Shoukaku have been unable to pinpoint what exactly they are doing. Now, a shinobi in their service, Akatsuki, has discovered vital intel -- a key -- to the "Sanctuary", a place never seen by outsiders, the root of myths and legends. They quickly head for their target.

As they approach the Mirror Sea, they become engulfed in a storm and separated. After nearly drowning, Zuikaku finds herself within the "Sanctuary", though alone and unable to contact Shoukaku.

What began as an investigation becomes a search-and-rescue mission as Zuikaku looks for her sister Shoukaku, opposed along the way by counterfeit copies of her friends, begining with Akatsuki.

In the darkness of the Shrine, Akagi and Kaga resent what they percieve as Zuikaku's attempt to hinder their reuninon with "That Person", Amagi. They are not very surprised to see her here, though, noting that "the bait [the key] really attracked that pest". To stall her they send out Ise and Hyuuga with several MPS.

After defeating the forces sent against her, Zuikaku recalls how she broke the news of finding the "Sanctuary" key and figures that the key was just a trap.

She next meets with the shrine maiden Yamashiro, who does her duty of gaurding the shrine with cheerful clumsiness. Zuikaku asks her where Shoukaku is; Yamashiro replies that she will tell Zuikaku if the latter can defeat her in combat. Yamashiro advises that she has been given a powerful upgrade, but before she can finish speaking, either her planes or Zuikaku attacked her.

Even the technology given by the Sirens cannot spare Yamashiro from utter defeat. After clumsily giving Zuikaku the information she needs, and extracting a promise from her to "save our futures" the latter heads into the shrine.

Akagi notes Zuikaku's heading towards the engraving room, and cannot understand why others in the Sakura Empire cannot comprehend "That Person's" will. Kaga urges her to not get upset, because the engraving process shouldn't take long now that the molds are set. Akagi decides to send Nowaki to stall for time.

Nowaki approaches her mission target, noting her size and power. But Nowaki also has her stats enhanced. She bluffs before the battle.

It turns out being a "lucky ship" was a scam. Zuikaku graciously says it's just that her own luck stat was much higher. She then interrogates Nowaki. Nowaki remarks that she is one of those who were brought to the shrine "in a daze", and that the long-missing Akagi and Kaga are researching Siren tech here in an effort to turn the war around. Zuikaku invites Nowaki to come help her save Shoukaku, but Nowaki declines, as it is Akagi who gave her hope.

As she enters the engraving room, Zuikaku remembers her recklessness at the Coral Sea. She had failed to keep back combat air patrol, and was forced to retreat into a rainsquall after the loss of Shouhou and the attack of enemy planes. Shoukaku promises to protect her, but admonishes her against recklessness. The present Zuikaku promises herself she will never let such a thing happen again.

Zuikaku finds her sister at last, except she's a pawn. Enraged, she demands to know why Akagi and Kaga want to do this. Akagi posits that one should offer exquisite sacrifices if one wants high-quality results. Kaga explains it's "for the Sakura Empire's future". Unconvinced by these lies, Zuikaku notes that they will even go so far as using their compatriots as guinea pigs to gain power. The power they seek is accursed, not from the sacred kami, and cannot deliver the Sakura Empire's future. Moreover, Zuikaku had anticipated their intentions from the beginning, but this time Akagi and Kaga have picked the wrong target. Akagi laughs and admonishes Zuikaku to think of how to defeat the opponents before her, then warns Zuikaku that she and Kaga will not hesitate to make a pawn of her too.

Zuikaku is forced to fight her bad seniors as well as the pawn of her sister, the latter being a very poor fighter. Outnumbered, she is almost overcome by the two alone. Kaga swoops in to claim her soul, but Shoukaku, now the real thing and a pawn no longer, recalls her promise to protect her sister, intervenes in the nick of time. The shock stops the fight, Akagi marvelling that the pawn could resist their will, and that even a pawn, bound by commands and limited intelligence, would choose to defend the one designated as her sister. Kaga muses that this may be an "Awakening Response", and Akagi bids her fall back and prioritize reporting the awakening of other pawns, should it occur. Zuikaku and Shoukaku reunite. The latter's fine now, but her head hurts, and she had had a long dream. Zuikaku apologizes for her lack of cautiousness and of maturity. Shoukaku responds by teasing her about Enterprise before reaffirming her promise to protect Zuikaku, then bidding her to singlemindedly concentrate on her goal (of defeating Enterprise). On a more serious note, she bids Zuikaku intercept Akagi and Kaga and inquire after their motives.

Akagi is elated at the prospect of receiving a reward of power for their experimental success. Kaga cautions that Zuikaku is following them, but Akagi says having a round with Zuikaku wouldn't hurt. Kaga remarks that Akagi hasn't changed a bit.

Akagi and Kaga had faked their deaths at Midway, but were alive here. Zuikaku demanded to know what else they were hiding. They argue once more over the validity of gaining power from the Sirens, till Zuikaku insists that such power can never defeat the Grey Ghost. Having pressed Akagi's berserk button, Kaga calls upon Zuikaku to show in battle whether she is "strong or weak".

Akagi laughs maniacally, wanting to crush the gleaming light of clashing souls in her hand. Kaga sighs as Akagi goes Akagi, and remarks that the show is over. Akagi sorts herself out and takes Kaga with her as she leaves for the next sanctuary. Zuikaku's protests are interrupted by the site recycling process beginning. Kaga waxes philosophical about the fixedness of fate, and wishes the protection of the gods on Zuikaku.

The "deities" appear, Observer and Tester. They are unimpressed by this minor test site, till Tester notes the report of an "Awakening Response". They discuss the data, but the results are inconclusive. More testing is needed, and Observer begins the next round of testing without hesitation.
