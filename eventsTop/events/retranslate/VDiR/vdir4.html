Note: the Azur Lane Wiki lists Kaga as the speaker of the first line. Nagami also follows the updated game lore, and shows Kaga as the first speaker. However, the original cutscene as shown by both Shoul and the Chinese wiki have the first sentence said by a speaker on the left side of the screen, and Akagi's dialog in this cutscene is on the left side of the screen. This means that (unless there was some kind of mistake) Akagi, not Kaga, said that first line.
