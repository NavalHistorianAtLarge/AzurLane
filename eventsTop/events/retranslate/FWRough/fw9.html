Azur Lane has elsewhere adopted the word "concept". "Meme" in this case means "a concept that is forefront in the mind". At higher dimensions, in simulations, as well as in the Wisdom Cube network, people can create things just by thinking about them. So it is with X -- by knowing about it, it comes to be. Any creature or thing that can have an effect on the world can be percieved as having a greater or lesser effect on the world. This creature or thing is a "meme". The more "aura" or known-ness across the multiverse or world the "meme" has, the stronger it becomes. The hardships of a post-apocolyptic universe intensifies the "aura" of any "meme" that survives/dominates that multiverse or world.
